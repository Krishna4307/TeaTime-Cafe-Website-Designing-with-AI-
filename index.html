About Us

Our Story

TeaTime Café is a cozy place where coffee lovers and tea enthusiasts come together. We believe every cup should bring comfort, happiness, and a little moment of relaxation.

From freshly brewed coffee to refreshing iced teas, we serve delicious beverages and freshly prepared snacks using quality ingredients. Established in 2021.

Our café is designed for friends, families, students, and professionals who want to enjoy great drinks in a relaxing atmosphere.

Our mission

Serve comforting, quality drinks and snacks in a warm space for everyone.

Our vision

To be the neighbourhood spot where every sip has a story.
